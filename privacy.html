Privacy policy

Secure Diary: Private Journal (package com.inquiry_sites.secure_diary)
Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version

Your diary stays on your phone. We do not run a server for your data, we do not have your entries, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking. We cannot read your diary and cannot recover it for you.

Who we are

Secure Diary is published by YOUR NAME OR COMPANY. Contact: tanmays9999@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your diary entries, mood, tags and photos. They are stored in an encrypted database and encrypted files on your device.
  • Your PIN or pattern, stored only as a salted hash. The key that protects your diary is held in the Android Keystore.
  • App settings, such as theme and auto-lock choice.
  • Optionally, the location you attach to an entry. This is saved only if you choose to add it, and it stays in that entry on your device.

None of this is sent to us.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to turn on backup, the app signs in to your Google account and uploads an encrypted backup file to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. The file is encrypted on your phone with a passphrase that only you know, before it leaves the device. We never receive the file or the passphrase, and the app cannot see your other Drive files. If you forget the passphrase, the backup cannot be recovered by anyone. Google's handling of your Drive and account is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Biometrics: to let you unlock with fingerprint or face. The app never sees your biometric data. Android handles it.
  • Location (optional): only when you tap to add a location to an entry.
  • Camera / photos: only when you add a picture to an entry.
  • Internet: only used for the optional Google Drive backup.

Data we collect and share

We collect no personal data, and we share no data with third parties. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting services.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete an entry, or empty the trash, at any time.
  • Use Settings → Delete all data to erase the diary from your phone.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes the data stored on your phone.
  • Delete your Drive backups from within the app's backup screen, or remove the app's data in your Google account settings.
  • Use Export diary to take a copy of your entries and photos.

Security

Entries and photos use strong encryption on the device, and backups use a passphrase-derived key. No system is perfectly secure, and if someone knows your PIN or pattern and has your unlocked phone, they can open the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 9 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, since we collect none.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
