Secure Diary: Private Journal

A private, encrypted diary for Android. No account, no ads, no tracking, and it works offline.

What it does

  • Lock your diary with a PIN, a pattern, or fingerprint / face unlock.
  • Your entries and photos are encrypted on your phone.
  • Add mood, tags, photos and (optionally) your location to each entry.
  • Search, calendar view, favourites, archive and trash.
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive, encrypted with a passphrase only you know.
  • Four colour themes, with light and dark modes.
